Research & Projects Breakdown

An overview of the core technical themes behind my public and experimental repositories:

1. Educational Ransomware & Hybrid Encryption Prototypes

Developing functional ransomware prototypes strictly for academic and defensive research purposes. This involves analyzing efficient directory traversal patterns, multi-threaded file enumeration, and implementing robust hybrid encryption schemes (AES-256 payloads secured via public-key cryptography) to understand how malware authors handle key exchanges and target isolation.

2. Cryptography: Classical Limitations & Post-Quantum (PQC) Shift

Exploring the cryptographic spectrum from classical and traditional ciphers to the emerging post-quantum era. Traditional asymmetric schemes (RSA, ECC, Diffie-Hellman) face severe vulnerabilities against scalable quantum attacks via Shor's algorithm. My research focuses on analyzing the architectural friction, performance overhead, and memory constraints involved in migrating toward NIST-standardized Post-Quantum Cryptography (lattice-based and hash-based signatures), alongside studying implementation flaws like cryptographic downgrades.
